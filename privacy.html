Privacy Policy

Athar Quraan · أثر القرآن — last updated 2 October 2026

In short

Athar Quraan does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Data kept on your device only

The following stays in the app’s storage on your phone and never leaves it:

  • Display, video and player preferences (theme, fonts, reciter, speed).
  • Your memorization plan and progress, and your daily adhkar counters.
  • Recitations, tafsir and videos you choose to download for offline use.
  • A background video or adhan recording you choose to import into the app.
  • Your location for prayer times and the Qibla, and your adhan and notification choices.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it.

Online services the app contacts

To stream recitations and load tafsir, the app requests public content from Quran.com, MP3Quran and EveryAyah, and adhkar recordings from HisnMuslim.com, and the King Fahd Complex mushaf page fonts and Arabic tafsir from the Quran Foundation (verses.quran.foundation). These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the content (for example, a surah number and reciter). Like any website, those services receive your IP address when your device connects to them; their own privacy policies apply. The Quran text, adhkar, hadith and nature videos are included in the app and work without a connection.

Location

If you choose “Use my location”, your position is read once on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and is named using a city list built into the app. Your coordinates are never sent anywhere. If you search for a city by name, the app first looks in its built-in list; when you are online it also sends only the text you typed to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to find smaller towns.

Permissions

  • Internet: to stream and download recitations and tafsir.
  • Notifications and foreground media playback: to show playback controls and keep a recitation playing while the screen is off or you use other apps.
  • Picture-in-picture: to keep the recitation video in a floating window when you leave the app.
  • Location (while using the app): optional, for prayer times and the Qibla compass; processed on the device only.
  • Alarms & reminders and run at startup: to play the adhan exactly at prayer time and to keep alerts after the phone restarts.
  • Motion / compass: to point the Qibla compass.
